Visiting this website

The hosting provider receives technical information such as your IP address, the requested resource, the time of the request and browser information when it delivers the site. Cookies and browser storage

This site keeps your cookie preference in your browser’s local storage so it can remember your selection. You can change it at any time in Cookie Settings or clear it by deleting this site's data in your browser. The site currently uses no optional cookies or analytics scripts.

Contacting TBench

When you contact us by email, we use your contact details and message to handle your request. This is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual or pre-contractual requests and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for other inquiries, based on our legitimate interest in responding. We keep the information for as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable retention obligations.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and, where applicable, portability. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw consent at any time where processing relies on consent. You can also complain to a supervisory authority, such as the Hamburg Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.
