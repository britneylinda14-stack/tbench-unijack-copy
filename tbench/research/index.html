RESEARCH / 01Autonomous UAV coordination

Swarm Flight Unit

Research into an optical system for coordinating a swarm of unmanned aircraft without relying on an external data source. The work was demonstrated at CES 2017 and Embedded World 2018 and later presented at ICCR 2024.

Research institution
Helmut Schmidt University Hamburg
Inventors
Jens Müller · Joachim Horn
Patent reference
DE 11 2016 005 353.3

RESEARCH / 02Uninterrupted torque transmission

Automated Switch Gear

The Horn–Müller–von der Wense (HMW) gearbox explores a way to sustain traction while shifting without adding cogwheels. The approach was presented at IEEE ICM 2025.

Research institution
Helmut Schmidt University Hamburg
Inventors
Joachim Horn · Jens Müller · Jens von der Wense
Patent reference
LU 93119 · EP 17 73 3441.4

RESEARCH / 03Modular micro-manufacturing

Function Integrated Mechanical Interface

An intelligent mechanical interface for tools, carriers and machines intended to reduce the effort required to reconfigure small modular machine tools. The project produced peer-reviewed publications and demonstrations.

Research institution
Helmut Schmidt University Hamburg
Inventors
Silka Grimske · Jens Storjohann · Jens Müller · Jens Wulfsberg

RESEARCH / 04Assistive navigation

Intelligent Blindman's Stick

An audio-based navigation aid designed to help visually impaired travelers locate doors on public transport. Developed in a research and youth-science context and recognized at ISEF 2018.

Research institution
Helmut Schmidt University Hamburg
Inventors
Nele Tornow · Fabian Rimmele · Jens Müller · Robert Weidner
Patent reference

RESEARCH / 05Assistive mechanical systems

Elastic Steerable Joint

A flexible, multi-directional joint concept for exoskeletons, developed to accommodate natural movement and help limit mechanical stress on the wearer.
